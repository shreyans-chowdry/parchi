Scalability

Cheap per order. Safe per shop.

~$0

for most order lines

The rules engine reads them on the device. The model sees only doubtful lines and photos.
1 call

per doubtful order

All unclear lines go to Claude together as one JSON request, so cost grows with confusion, not volume.
RLS

on every table

Each wholesaler's catalog, customers and bills are isolated by business_id in Postgres.
Queue

behind WhatsApp

The webhook stores each message once and enqueues it in pgmq. Morning rushes never block.
pg_trgm

for big catalogs

The database pre-filters a dozen candidates, the engine scores them. pgvector is ready as a fallback.
Atomic

bill numbers and stock

A per-year counter and a stock trigger run in the same transaction as the invoice.

The wedge

Wholesale districts where orders already run on WhatsApp. One distributor brings its whole retailer network.

Why it spreads

Every invoice goes back to a retailer on WhatsApp. Retailers who get clean invoices ask their other suppliers for the same.

Why it pays

One saved hour a day at a counter is worth far more than $19 a month, and our cost per order stays close to zero.
